## What Every Plan Includes

All Paperlink AI plans — from Starter to Scale — come with the same core capabilities. You never pay extra for the following:

- **Unlimited sites** — embed your chat widget on as many websites, apps, or portals as you need
- **RAG-powered AI** — every bot uses retrieval-augmented generation to answer questions accurately from your documents
- **Real-time analytics dashboard** — track conversations, response accuracy, and user engagement across all your bots
- **24/7 support** — reach the team any time via live chat in the dashboard or email at support@paperlink.ai
- **99.9% uptime SLA** — your bots stay live and responsive when your customers need them most

## Free Trial

Every new Paperlink AI account starts with a **3-day free trial** — no credit card required, no strings attached. During the trial you get full access to Growth-tier features so you can build a real bot, embed it, and see it answer customer questions before you spend a cent.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Visit [app.paperlink.ai/sign-up](https://pl-dev-web.nimishnandwana.workers.dev/sign-up) and sign in with Google, Microsoft, or your email and password. Your 3-day trial starts immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build and embed your first bot">
    Upload your documents, configure your bot, and paste the embed snippet on your site — all within minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan before your trial ends">
    You'll receive an email reminder before your trial expires. Select a plan from **Dashboard → Account → Billing** to keep your bots live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  If your trial ends before you select a plan, your bots are paused — not deleted. Your uploaded documents and bot configuration are preserved for 14 days so you can pick up right where you left off once you subscribe.
</Info>

## Monthly Reply Overages

Each plan includes a fixed number of **monthly replies** — a reply is counted each time a visitor sends a message and your bot returns an AI-generated answer. The counter resets on your billing renewal date every month.

When you reach your monthly reply limit, your bots stop responding to new questions until your next billing cycle begins. Visitors will see a polite fallback message letting them know the assistant is temporarily unavailable.

**To keep your bots responding without interruption, upgrade your plan before you hit the limit.** Head to [managing your subscription](/billing/managing-subscription) for step-by-step upgrade instructions. You can also monitor reply consumption in real time from the **Analytics** section of your dashboard so you're never caught off guard.

<Note>
  All Paperlink AI plans are billed **month-to-month** with no long-term contract required. Pricing shown on this page reflects monthly billing. If you're interested in annual billing options or volume discounts, contact the team at support@paperlink.ai and they'll be happy to discuss what works for your budget.
</Note>
