Paperlink AI transforms the documents your business already has — contracts, policies, manuals, FAQs — into a smart, conversational assistant that your customers can interact with around the clock. Whether you're a SaaS startup drowning in support tickets or a healthcare clinic fielding the same policy questions repeatedly, Paperlink AI puts accurate answers on your website without hiring extra staff or rebuilding your knowledge base from scratch.

## What Is Paperlink AI?

Paperlink AI is a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) powered Q&A engine built for businesses that want instant, document-grounded answers — not hallucinated responses from a generic chatbot. You upload your existing documents (PDFs, Word files, or plain text), and Paperlink AI indexes their content into a secure knowledge base. When a visitor asks a question, the engine retrieves the most relevant passages from your documents and synthesizes a precise, cited answer in real time.

Every bot you build is delivered as an **embeddable chat widget** — a single `<script>` snippet you paste onto any webpage. There's nothing to install, no backend to manage, and no AI infrastructure to maintain. You get a production-ready assistant in minutes, not months.

Key technical facts at a glance:

- **Supported formats:** PDF, Word (.docx), and plain text files
- **Document capacity:** up to 5,000 pages per bot
- **Response time:** under 2 seconds on average
- **Accuracy rate:** 98% across 2 million+ questions answered

## Who Uses Paperlink AI?

Paperlink AI is purpose-built for any team that has documents and gets questions about them. Common adopters include:

- **SaaS companies** — turn product documentation and help articles into an always-on support assistant
- **Healthcare clinics** — let patients self-serve insurance, billing, and policy questions securely
- **Legal firms** — give clients instant answers about intake processes, fees, and standard agreements
- **HR teams** — deploy a policy bot that fields employee questions about benefits, leave, and procedures
- **E-commerce brands** — surface shipping policies, return rules, and product specs directly in the buying flow
- **Real estate agencies** — help buyers and sellers navigate contracts, disclosures, and listing details

If your business has documents and your customers have questions, Paperlink AI is built for you.
