The Paperlink AI assistant is what your website visitors interact with directly. From their perspective, it's a familiar chat interface that appears on your site and answers questions instantly — no forms to fill out, no wait queues, no hunting through documentation. Behind that simple experience is a RAG-powered engine searching your documents in real time, retrieving the right information, and returning a clear answer in under two seconds. This page explains how the assistant behaves, what it's capable of, and how it fits into your customer experience.

## The Chat Widget

When you embed Paperlink AI on your website, a chat widget appears in the corner of your page — typically the bottom right. Visitors click to open it and start asking questions immediately, without any login or setup on their end.

The widget is added to your site with a single code snippet that you copy from your bot's **Embed** settings. Once added, the widget loads asynchronously, so it never slows down your page. You can configure the widget's name, greeting message, and accent color to match your brand.

The widget is fully responsive and works across desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers without any additional configuration.

## How Answers Are Structured

Paperlink AI does not paste raw paragraphs from your documents into the chat window. Instead, it synthesizes the relevant information into a clear, direct response formatted for conversational reading. Answers are concise, actionable, and written in plain language — even when the source document uses technical or legal terminology.

Every response is generated in **under two seconds** from the moment a visitor submits their question. That speed ensures the experience feels immediate rather than transactional, keeping visitors engaged and reducing the likelihood of them abandoning the page to search elsewhere.

Where appropriate, the assistant structures longer answers using bullet points or numbered steps, making complex information easy to scan. Visitors can ask follow-up questions in the same conversation, and the assistant maintains context within the session.

## Handling Unknown Questions

When a visitor asks a question that falls outside your uploaded documents, the assistant does not guess or fabricate an answer. Instead, it responds honestly — letting the visitor know the information isn't available in the current knowledge base — and can optionally direct them to your support team or a contact page.

This behavior is intentional. Visitors should always be able to trust the answers they receive. A clear "I don't know" is far more valuable than a confident but incorrect response.

## Multi-Language Support

Paperlink AI supports conversations in multiple languages. Visitors can ask questions in their preferred language, and the assistant will respond in kind — drawing from the same underlying documents regardless of the language the question was asked in.

<Info>
  Multi-language support works best when your source documents are in English or when you upload translations of your documents for specific languages. The assistant can interpret questions in other languages, but the accuracy of its responses depends on the content available in your knowledge base.
</Info>

## Always-On Availability

Your Paperlink AI assistant operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — including weekends, holidays, and outside your team's working hours. Visitors get answers immediately regardless of when they visit your site.

All plans include a **99.9% uptime SLA**, meaning your assistant is reliably available even during peak traffic periods. There are no maintenance windows that take the widget offline, and infrastructure scaling is handled automatically — you don't need to do anything to prepare for traffic spikes.

<Tip>
  Use your bot's always-on availability to handle the questions your support team answers repeatedly during off-hours. According to Paperlink AI usage data, bots reduce inbound support tickets by an average of **63%** — and a significant share of those deflections happen outside standard business hours when live agents aren't available.
</Tip>
