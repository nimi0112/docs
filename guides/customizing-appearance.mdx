Your chat widget is a visible part of your website's interface, and it should feel like it belongs there. Paperlink AI gives you full control over the widget's visual presentation — from brand colors and your company logo to the welcome message a visitor sees the moment the chat panel opens. You can make every customization directly in the dashboard using a visual editor, preview changes in real time before they go live, and push updates to all your embedded sites simultaneously without touching a single line of code.

## Finding Appearance Settings

All visual customization options live in one place:

1. Open the **Dashboard** and select the bot you want to customize.
2. Go to **Bot Settings → Appearance**.
3. The left panel shows all customization controls; the right panel shows a live preview of your widget as you make changes.

Every change you make updates the preview instantly. Click **Save Changes** when you're happy with the result — your live embedded widget updates automatically within a few seconds, everywhere it's installed.

## Saving and Previewing Changes

The **Appearance** editor shows a live preview of your widget as you adjust settings — no need to save and refresh to see how changes look. The preview reflects exactly what a visitor will see when the widget is embedded on your site, including the welcome message, colors, avatar, and theme.

When you're satisfied with your adjustments, click **Save Changes**. Updates propagate to your live embedded widget automatically — there's no need to update your embed snippet or redeploy your site. Visitors who already have your page open will see the changes on their next page load.

<Tip>
  **Match your primary color exactly for significantly higher widget engagement.** Widgets that blend naturally into a site's design receive more clicks and longer conversations than widgets that look like a third-party add-on. Use your brand's official hex color code rather than approximating by eye — you can usually find it in your brand guidelines, your CSS variables, or by using a browser color picker extension on your own site.
</Tip>

## Per-Bot Appearance

Appearance settings are configured **per bot**, not per site. If you embed the same bot on multiple websites, all those sites display the same widget design. This is intentional — it ensures consistent branding across your web presence.

If you need different widget designs for different sites (for example, a different color scheme for a white-label partner site), create a separate bot for each site and configure its appearance independently. Each bot has its own embed snippet, knowledge base, and appearance settings. See [Managing Multiple Bots](/guides/managing-multiple-bots) to learn how to set up and organize multiple bots.
