## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload your documents">
    Drag and drop PDFs, contracts, policies, or any text-based files directly into your Paperlink AI dashboard. The engine supports up to 5,000 pages per bot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI learns and structures your content">
    Paperlink AI's RAG engine extracts key information, indexes your documents, and builds a knowledge structure that understands context and intent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy and go live">
    Embed the chat widget on your website with a single line of code. Your visitors can now get instant, accurate answers from your documents — 24/7.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track performance">
    Monitor conversations, response accuracy, and user engagement from your real-time analytics dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
