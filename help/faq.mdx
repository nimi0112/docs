Whether you're just getting started or have been using Paperlink AI for a while, this page covers the most common questions from our customers — from uploading documents and deploying your widget to security, customization, and plan limits.

## General

<Accordion title="How fast can I deploy?">
  You can go from sign-up to a live chat widget in under 10 minutes. Upload your documents, configure your bot, and paste the embed script into your website — no developer required. All plans come with a 3-day free trial so you can test the full experience before committing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use the same bot on multiple websites?">
  Yes. All Paperlink AI plans include unlimited sites. Your embed code works on any number of domains, so you can deploy the same bot across a landing page, a help center, and a customer portal without any additional cost or configuration.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do you support multiple languages?">
  Yes. Paperlink AI can understand and respond to questions in multiple languages, provided your knowledge base documents contain relevant content in those languages. The AI will match the language of the user's question as closely as possible based on available content.
</Accordion>

## Documents & Knowledge Base

<Accordion title="What file types are supported?">
  Paperlink AI supports the following file formats:

  - **PDF** (`.pdf`) — the most common format for policies, contracts, and manuals
  - **Microsoft Word** (`.docx`) — ideal for internal documentation and structured guides
  - **Plain text** (`.txt`) — useful for FAQs, changelogs, and lightweight content

  Make sure your files contain extractable text. Scanned image-only PDFs are not fully supported (see below).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How many documents can I upload?">
  Each bot supports up to 5,000 pages of content. Your plan determines how many total knowledge pages you can use across all bots:

  | Plan    | Knowledge Pages |
  |---------|----------------|
  | Starter | 500 pages       |
  | Growth  | 5,000 pages     |
  | Scale   | 25,000 pages    |

  If you need more capacity, you can upgrade your plan at any time from the billing settings in your dashboard.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if my documents are poorly formatted?">
  Paperlink AI handles most PDFs and Word documents well, especially those with clear headings and structured content. For best results, use documents with consistent formatting, labeled sections, and extractable text.

  Scanned image-only PDFs may result in lower answer accuracy because the AI relies on text it can extract directly from the file. If you have a scanned document, try re-uploading a text-based version — for example, a searchable PDF exported from the original source — to improve results.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I have multiple bots?">
  Yes. You can create multiple bots, each with its own knowledge base, appearance settings, and embed code. The number of bots available depends on your plan:

  | Plan    | Bots Included |
  |---------|--------------| 
  | Starter | 1 bot         |
  | Growth  | 3 bots        |
  | Scale   | 10 bots       |

  This lets you build separate assistants for different products, departments, or audiences — all managed from one dashboard.
</Accordion>

## Replies & Plan Limits

<Accordion title="What happens when I use up my monthly replies?">
  When you reach your plan's monthly reply limit, your bot will stop responding to new questions until your billing cycle resets. Your widget will remain visible on your site, but users will see a message indicating that responses are temporarily unavailable.

  You can upgrade your plan at any time from the billing settings in your dashboard — your new reply limit takes effect immediately. Reply counts reset on the first day of each billing cycle.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What are the reply limits for each plan?">
  Each plan includes a monthly reply allocation that resets at the start of every billing cycle:

  | Plan    | Monthly Replies |
  |---------|----------------|
  | Starter | 3,000 replies   |
  | Growth  | 10,000 replies  |
  | Scale   | 40,000 replies  |

  If you regularly reach your limit before the month ends, upgrading to the next tier restores your reply count immediately upon processing.
</Accordion>

## Security & Privacy

<Accordion title="Is my data secure?">
  Yes. Paperlink AI uses end-to-end encryption for all data in transit and at rest. Your documents are **never** used to train AI models — they are stored solely to power your bot's responses. You can delete your data at any time directly from the dashboard, and deletion is permanent and immediate.

  <Note>
    For more detail on our data handling practices, refer to the Paperlink AI Privacy Policy linked in the dashboard footer.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

## Customization & Embedding

<Accordion title="Can I customize the chatbot UI?">
  Yes. From the **Appearance** settings in your bot dashboard, you can customize:

  - **Color** — match your brand's primary color
  - **Position** — bottom-right or bottom-left of the screen
  - **Bot name** — displayed in the chat header
  - **Avatar** — upload a custom icon or logo
  - **Welcome message** — the first message users see when the widget opens
  - **Theme** — light or dark mode

  Changes are reflected in your embedded widget in real time without needing to update your embed code.
</Accordion>
