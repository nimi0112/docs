The Paperlink AI support team is here to help you get the most out of your account. Whether you have a quick question or need in-depth help troubleshooting an issue, you have two ways to reach us — choose the channel that fits your situation best.

## Support Channels

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Email" icon="envelope">
    Send your question to **support@paperlink.ai** any time. Best for non-urgent issues, billing questions, or anything that benefits from a detailed written response. We typically reply within 2 hours during business hours.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Live Chat" icon="message">
    Start a conversation directly inside your Paperlink AI dashboard. Live chat is available **24/7** and connects you with a support agent in real time — ideal for quick questions while you're actively working on your bot.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Response Times

Depending on when and how you contact us, here's what to expect:

| Channel    | Availability                        | Typical Response Time                  |
|------------|-------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Email      | Any time                            | Within 2 hours (Mon–Fri, 9 AM–6 PM EST)|
| Live Chat  | 24/7 in the dashboard               | Within a few minutes                   |

  Outside of business hours, live chat remains available for real-time assistance. Email responses sent after 6 PM EST will be answered the next business morning.
</Info>

## When Contacting Support

Providing the right details upfront helps us resolve your issue faster — and avoids unnecessary back-and-forth. Before reaching out, have the following ready:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Include your account email">
    Share the email address associated with your Paperlink AI account so we can pull up your details immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what you expected vs. what happened">
    Be specific: tell us what you were trying to do, what you expected to see, and what actually happened instead. The more context you provide, the faster we can diagnose the problem.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach screenshots or recordings">
    If you're experiencing a visual issue — a widget not appearing, an error message, or unexpected bot behavior — a screenshot or short screen recording is extremely helpful. Attach these directly to your email or share them in live chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Note any recent changes">
    Let us know if you recently uploaded new documents, changed your embed code, updated your plan, or made any other changes before the issue appeared. This helps us narrow down the cause quickly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  For enterprise pricing or custom integrations, email support@paperlink.ai with the subject line **"Enterprise Inquiry"** and a member of our team will get back to you within one business day.
</Note>
